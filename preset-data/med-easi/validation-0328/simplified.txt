Bacteria commonly spread up the genital tract and infect the tubes that connect the ovaries to the uterus (fallopian tubes). This infection, called salpingitis, causes severe lower abdominal pain, especially during intercourse. In some women, the infection spreads to the lining of the abdominal cavity (peritoneum), causing peritonitis or pelvic inflammatory disease, which may cause severe pain in the lower abdomen. Women who have had pelvic inflammatory disease have an increased risk of infertility and mislocated (ectopic) pregnancies, which can cause dangerous internal bleeding.
