In most children, the first evidence of the cancer is a firm lump or problems related to an organ affected by the cancer, such as the following: Eyes: Tearing, eye pain, or a bulging eye; Nose and throat: Nasal congestion, a change in the voice, or a nasal discharge that contains mucus and pus; Genital or urinary tract: Abdominal pain, a lump in the abdomen that can be felt, difficulty urinating, and blood in the urine; Limbs: Firm lumps on the arms or legs.
