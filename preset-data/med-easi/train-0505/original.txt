Usually, children present with a firm, palpable mass or with organ dysfunction due to impingement on the organ by the cancer. Orbital and nasopharyngeal cancers may cause tearing, eye pain, or proptosis. Nasopharyngeal cavity cancers may cause nasal congestion, a change in voice, or mucopurulent discharge. Genitourinary cancers cause abdominal pain, a palpable abdominal mass, difficulty urinating, and hematuria. Extremity cancers appear as firm, indiscrete masses anywhere on the arms or legs.
