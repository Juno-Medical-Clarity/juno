Symptoms of postpartum depression may be similar to those of major depression and may include Extreme sadness; Guilt; Uncontrollable crying; Insomnia or increased sleep; Loss of appetite or overeating; Irritability and anger; Headaches and body aches and pains; Extreme fatigue; Unrealistic worries about or disinterest in the baby; A feeling of being incapable of caring for the baby or of being inadequate as a mother; Fear of harming the baby; Suicidal ideation; Anxiety or panic attacks.
