Symptoms of postpartum depression may include Extreme sadness; Frequent crying; Mood swings; Irritability and anger. Less common symptoms include Extreme fatigue; Sleep problems (too much or too little); Headaches and body aches; Loss of interest in sex and other activities; Anxiety or panic attacks; Loss of appetite or overeating; Difficulty functioning; Lack of interest in or unreasonable worries about the baby; Feelings of inadequacy or hopelessness; Guilt about having these feelings; Fear of harming the baby; Suicidal thoughts.
