Infectious mononucleosis (IM; also known as mono, glandular fever, Pfeiffer 's disease, Filatov 's disease, and sometimes colloquially as the kissing disease (from its oral transmission) is an infectious, widespread viral disease caused by the Epstein-Barr virus (EBV), one type of herpes virus, against which over 90 % of adults are likely to have acquired immunity by the age of 40.
