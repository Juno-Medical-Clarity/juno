If the examination does not suggest a cause of a cough and no warning signs are present, many doctors try giving people a drug to treat one of two common causes of cough: An antihistamine/decongestant combination or a nasal corticosteroid or nasal muscarinic antagonist spray (for postnasal drip); A proton pump inhibitor or histamine-2 (H2) blocker (for gastroesophageal reflux disease).
