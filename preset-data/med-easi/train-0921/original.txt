A mnemonic that can be used to remember the characteristics of histrionic personality disorder is shortened as `` PRAISE ME '': Provocative ( or seductive ) behavior Relationships are considered more intimate than they actually are Attention-seeking Influenced easily by others or circumstances Speech ( style ) wants to impress; lacks detail Emotional lability; shallowness Make-up; physical appearance is used to draw attention to self Exaggerated emotions; theatrical Little research has been done to find evidence of what causes histrionic personality disorder and from where it stems.
