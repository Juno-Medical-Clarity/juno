If children do not move their bowels when the natural urge comes, the rectum eventually stretches to accommodate the stool. When the rectum has stretched, the urge to have a BM lessens, and more and more stool accumulates and hardens. A vicious circle of worsening constipation may result. If the accumulated stool hardens, it sometimes blocks the passage of other stool condition called fecal impaction. Looser stool from above the hardened stool may leak around the impaction into the child 's underwear and lead to stool incontinence (encopresis).
