# SOAP Notes — Sample 0454
# Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SubashNeupane/dataset_SOAP_summary

Subjective:
- Patient diagnosed with acute on chronic right slipped capital femoral epiphysis.
- History of surgery: underwent situ pinning.
- Patient is 13 years old.
- Concern about the screw now going into the hip joint.
- Inquiry about risks of infection from the upcoming surgery; patient was informed that the risk is less than one percent.
- Concern regarding recovery, including potential changes in sensation, motion, and stiffness post-surgery.
- Patient expresses desire to return to playing sports with friends.

Objective:
- X-rays indicate that the screw is now going into the hip joint.

Assessment:
- Need for another operation to remove the current screw and replace it with a shorter one to prevent further joint damage.

Plan:
- Schedule surgery to remove the screw and place a shorter one.
- Use anesthesia during the procedure.
- Discussed recovery expectations and potential risks (e.g., changes in sensation, motion, stiffness, and bleeding). 
- Patient and family agreed to proceed with the surgery.
