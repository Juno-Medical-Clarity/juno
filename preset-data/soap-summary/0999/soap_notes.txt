# SOAP Notes — Sample 0999
# Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SubashNeupane/dataset_SOAP_summary

Subjective:
- Patient is a 10-year-old boy who has been experiencing stiffness in his left elbow.
- He has a history of a fracture dislocation of the left elbow and a fracture of the neck of the right glenoid from an incident while riding quads on October 10, 2007.
- He is currently undergoing physical therapy (PT) and using a Dynasplint.
- Family expresses concern about the risk of infection associated with the upcoming surgery and whether it will fix his pain, acknowledging that there are no guarantees regarding pain relief.
- Family desires for the patient to be able to play sports with his friends.

Objective:
- The left elbow fracture is noted to have healed well.
- Neurological examination shows the patient is intact in terms of movement and reflexes in his wrist and hand.

Assessment:
- The left elbow fracture has healed, but the patient is experiencing stiffness.
- Planned surgery to remove hardware from the left elbow to potentially decrease irritation with elbow extension.

Plan:
- Schedule surgery to remove hardware from the left elbow.
- Use prophylactic antibiotics during surgery to minimize the risk of infection.
- Discuss risks of surgery with family, including bleeding, changes in sensation and motion, failure to remove hardware, failure to relieve pain, and continued postoperative stiffness.
- Ensure that anesthesia will be used so the patient will not feel anything during the procedure.
