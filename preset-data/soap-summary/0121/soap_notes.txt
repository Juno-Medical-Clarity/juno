PMHx:

FHx:
- No family history of lung or heart conditions

SocHx:
- Lives at home with parents and younger sister
- No environmental exposures (mold, asbestos, etc.)
- No recent travel

Objective:
- Vitals signs: Temp 101 F

Assessment:
- Acute viral upper respiratory tract infection
- Suspected COVID-19

Plan:
- COVID-19 testing, isolate for 14 days pending results (including from sister)
- Return precautions: Worsening fever, neurological symptoms (seizures, weakness, dizziness, syncope), respiratory distress
- If COVID-19 negative, consider alternate viral etiology vs. atypical bacterial pneumonia vs. asthma exacerbation
