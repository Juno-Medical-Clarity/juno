PMHx:

- Denies asthma, other medical conditions
- Father had MI last year in his 50s, now seeing cardiologists; paternal grandfather died young from accident, unknown cardiac hx

SocHx:
- Lives alone in London for MSW program
- Smokes 5 cigs/day, previously ~10/day for 2 yrs; began smoking at age 18
- No known sick contacts

Medications:
- Multivitamin
- Denies other medications

Objective:
- Vitals not obtained

Assessment:
- Atypical chest pain in a young adult smoker with FHx of premature CAD
- Likely musculoskeletal or anxiety-related, but cannot rule out cardiac etiology without further evaluation

Plan:
- Obtain vitals including BP, HR, RR, SpO2
- Perform thorough CV & pulm exam

- Follow up after evaluation to review results & determine next steps
