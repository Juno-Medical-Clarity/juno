# SOAP Notes — Sample 0780
# Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SubashNeupane/dataset_SOAP_summary

Subjective:
- Patient is a ten-year-old boy.
- Complains of tightness in the back of his legs.
- Has been using a walker since he started walking at about one year old.
- Current difficulty in flexing legs at the popliteal angle; can flex to about 20 degrees (normal is 90 degrees).
- Exhibits toe walking.

Objective:
- Popliteal angle flexion limited to 20 degrees bilaterally (normal is 90 degrees).
- Observed toe walking pattern during ambulation.

Assessment:
- Diagnosis of hamstring tightness.
- Consideration for surgical intervention to address tightness and toe walking.

Plan:
- Recommend surgical intervention on hamstrings.
- Discussed risks of surgery: low risk of infection (less than 1%), anesthesia risks, potential bleeding, changes in sensation, possible failure to correct toe walking, and stiffness after casting.
- Confirmed that the patient will be under anesthesia during the procedure.
- Family agrees to proceed with the surgical procedure.
