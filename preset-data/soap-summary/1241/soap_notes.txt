# SOAP Notes — Sample 1241
# Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SubashNeupane/dataset_SOAP_summary

Subjective:
- Patient is a 13-year-old female who fell off a swing at school.
- Reports severe pain in the left elbow.
- Pain is new and associated with burning and tingling feelings in the arm, which were not present before the attempted reduction.
- The pain is worsening.
- Family expresses concerns about the risks of infection associated with surgery.

Objective:
- X-rays show an incarcerated medial epicondyle fracture and a mildly angulated radial shaft break.

Assessment:
- Left elbow fracture (incarcerated medial epicondyle fracture and radial shaft fracture).
- Potential ulnar nerve compression due to the fracture and attempted reduction.

Plan:
- Perform Open Reduction and Internal Fixation (ORIF) procedure as soon as possible.
- Reduce the radial shaft fracture during the same procedure.
- Use prophylactic antibiotics to minimize infection risk.
- Discuss potential risks of the surgery including less than 1% infection risk, bleeding, changes in sensation and motion, and hardware failure.
- Inform family that the patient will be asleep during the procedure and that hardware will need to be removed in about a year or two after healing.
