# SOAP Notes — Sample 0480
# Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SubashNeupane/dataset_SOAP_summary

Subjective:
- Patient is 13 years old and has had foot problems all her life.
- Had bilateral feet corrections at age one.
- Reports a lot of pain in her heel.
- Expressed a desire to return to playing sports with friends.
- Family member inquired about the risks of infection and other complications related to surgery.

Objective:
- Significant calcaneal valgus deformity observed.
- Radiographs indicate a collapsed spinal arch.

Assessment:
- Diagnosis: Calcaneal valgus deformity.
- Surgical recommendation: Calcaneal osteotomy and Achilles lengthening procedure.
- Risks discussed include infection, bleeding, changes in sensation, hardware failure, nonunion, failure to correct deformity, and potential need for further surgeries, though these are stated to be rare.

Plan:
- Proceed with calcaneal osteotomy and Achilles lengthening procedure.
- Patient will require nonweightbearing status for a minimum of six weeks post-surgery.
- A brace will be needed for at least six months, contingent on adherence to post-operative care.
- Prescribe antibiotics to minimize infection risk during surgery.
- Follow-up appointments to monitor recovery and adherence to post-operative instructions.
