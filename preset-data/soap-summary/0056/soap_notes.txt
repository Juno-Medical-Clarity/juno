Assessment:

1. Transient synovitis (most likely given recent viral illness, age, presentation)
2. Septic arthritis (less likely, but cannot rule out without further workup)
3. Legg-Calve-Perthes disease (less likely given acute onset)

Plan:
- Trial of ibuprofen for pain control
- Close follow up in 1-2 days to reassess
- Counsel on signs/symptoms to watch for that would warrant earlier reassessment (high fever, red/hot/swollen joint, worsening pain/inability to bear weight)
- If no improvement or worsening, consider workup for septic arthritis vs Legg-Calve-Perthes
