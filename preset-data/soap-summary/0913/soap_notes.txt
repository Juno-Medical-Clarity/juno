# SOAP Notes — Sample 0913
# Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SubashNeupane/dataset_SOAP_summary

###Subjective:**
- Patient is an 84-year-old female.
- Symptoms:
 - Experienced a fall yesterday on the driveway, hitting her left side against a rock.
 - Felt like she had just run a race after the fall, with a pounding heart.
- Duration: Symptoms occurred immediately after the fall but resolved quickly; she felt normal afterward and has not experienced the rapid heartbeat again.
- Associated symptoms: None mentioned after the fall; no chest pain or shortness of breath before or after the fall.
- Relevant medical history:
 - High blood pressure (hypertension).
 - History of severe tricuspid regurgitation, mild pulmonary hypertension, mild aortic stenosis, and previously moderate mitral regurgitation.
- Family history and allergies: Not mentioned.
- Concerns: Patient expressed confusion about heart conditions and terminology.

Objective:
- Vital signs: 
 - Heart rate was very high during the emergency room visit.
 - Cardizem was administered and stopped when the heart rate came down to the fifties.
- Electrocardiogram (ECG) findings:
 - Rapid heart rate and abnormal pacemaker activity noted in initial ECG.
 - Subsequent ECG showed normal rate with frequent early heartbeats.
- Laboratory results: 
 - Potassium level: 3.1.

Assessment:
- Rapid atrial tachyarrhythmia noted during the emergency room visit.
- Need to check for atrial fibrillation due to the fall and high heart rate.
- No ongoing symptoms of chest pain or shortness of breath.

Plan:
- Further evaluation for atrial fibrillation.
- Monitor potassium levels and manage as necessary.
- Patient education regarding her heart conditions and terminology used in her medical care.
