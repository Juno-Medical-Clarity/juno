Subjective:

- Describes balancing problems.
- Previous fall occurred approximately a week and a half ago.

Assessment:

- Need for further tests to determine if the masses are malignant.

Plan:
- Run additional tests.
- Consider performing a biopsy to assess the nature of the clinoid masses.
