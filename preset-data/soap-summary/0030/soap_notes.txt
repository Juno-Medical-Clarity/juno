Subjective:
- Left knee pain for 3-4 months, worsening over the past few weeks
- Initially only during activity, now also at rest (sharp pain with activity, dull pain at rest)
- Pain worse with stairs, running (sharp, stabbing pain with foot strike)
- Pain severity: 6/10
- Previous episodes: Similar pain many years ago as a rower in undergrad, resolved with stretching and strengthening
- Impact on daily activities: Pain noticeable even with walking

PMHx:
- Otherwise healthy, no diagnosed medical conditions
- No hospitalizations or surgeries
- Medications: Multivitamin
- Immunizations up to date
- No allergies
- No FHx of musculoskeletal issues
- Father has hypertension and hypercholesterolemia
- Social Hx: Lives alone, recently completed master's degree
- Denies smoking, occasional marijuana use in the past, no other recreational drugs

Objective:
- No visible redness, swelling, bruising, muscle atrophy, deformities or skin changes
- No temperature changes between right and left knee
- No pain with palpation of kneecap, medial/lateral ligaments, quadriceps, patellar tendon, proximal tibia/fibula
- Pain with palpation of lateral joint line
- Pain with standing from seated position and sitting back down
- No pain with knee flexion or extension
- Tightness and discomfort with hip abduction
- Positive Ober test (inability to adduct thigh, leg "floating")

Assessment:
- Iliotibial band (ITB) syndrome

Plan:
- Activity modification (switch from running to swimming)
- Ice/heat, taping, stretching
- Ibuprofen for temporary pain relief
- Referral to physiotherapy for strengthening and range of motion
- Consider ultrasound therapy, muscle stimulation, shockwave therapy if conservative management ineffective
- Surgical management as last resort
