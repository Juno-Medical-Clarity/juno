# SOAP Notes — Sample 0481
# Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SubashNeupane/dataset_SOAP_summary

Subjective:
- Patient is a five-year-old male.
- Fell onto his right arm on December 5, 2007.
- Parents report that he was treated in the emergency department (ED) after the fall.
- ED indicated he had complete fractures of both bones in the arm.
- Parents expressed a preference for casting over surgery initially but ultimately decided they would like to proceed with surgery after discussing options and risks.

Objective:
- Physical examination findings indicate shortening bayonet apposition of the right arm.

Assessment:
- Complete fractures of both bones in the right arm.
- Shortening bayonet apposition noted.

Plan:
- Proceed with surgical intervention for fracture repair.
- Discussed risks of surgery, including infection (low risk < 1%), bleeding, changes in sensation and motion, hardware failure, need for later hardware removal, and cast tightness.
- Administer antibiotics to control for infection during the procedure.
- Confirmed patient will be under anesthesia during the surgery.
