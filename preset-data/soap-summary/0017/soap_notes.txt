# SOAP Notes — Sample 0017
# Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SubashNeupane/dataset_SOAP_summary

Subjective:
- Presenting with right wrist pain after tripping and falling onto hand last night at 10 PM (fell from standing height onto concrete, landing on right hand)
- Sharp pain at base of thumb and wrist (rates pain 3/10 at rest, 9/10 with movement)
- Swelling and bruising present at base of thumb on dorsal aspect of wrist
- Pain worse with wrist flexion/extension and thumb movement, better with NSAIDs (anti-inflammatories) 
- Difficulty with ADLs (activities of daily living) due to pain (e.g. texting)
- Denies head injury, LOC (loss of consciousness), numbness/tingling in hand, fever, chills, headache, dizziness, vision/hearing changes, GI (gastrointestinal) symptoms

PMHx:
- Asthma (well-controlled, uses orange inhaler - Bayme)
- Meds: multivitamin, vitamin D
- Surgical Hx: tonsillectomy, wisdom teeth extraction, C-section (no complications)
- No prior right hand injuries or fractures

FHx:
- Uncle and grandfather with MI (myocardial infarction) in 60s

SocHx: 
- Works in restaurant, recently restarted job (concerned about impact of injury on work duties)
- Lives alone in apartment
- Denies smoking (never smoked due to asthma)
- Alcohol: 2-3 glasses of wine/day (2 bottles/week)
- Denies recreational drug use

Objective:
- Swelling and bruising at base of thumb on dorsal wrist (not warm to touch)
- Tenderness to palpation at base of thumb 
- Reduced thumb ROM (range of motion), unable to make full fist
- Pain with wrist flexion/extension
- Distal joints of thumb non-tender
- No pain in fingers or elbow

Assessment:
- Suspect scaphoid fracture given mechanism of injury (FOOSH - fall on outstretched hand) and exam findings (snuffbox tenderness, swelling/bruising at thumb base, pain with wrist ROM)

Plan:
- X-ray right wrist and thumb to evaluate for fracture
- Splint or cast depending on X-ray results
- Follow-up based on X-ray findings to determine duration of immobilization
- Counseled on reducing alcohol intake to max 2 glasses wine/day
