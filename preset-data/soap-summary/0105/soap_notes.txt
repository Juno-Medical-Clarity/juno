# SOAP Notes — Sample 0105
# Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SubashNeupane/dataset_SOAP_summary

Subjective:
- Presents with chronic cough that has been worsening over the past few years (cough ongoing for a few years, slowly worsening)
- Cough productive of green, sometimes yellowish sputum (a couple of Kleenexes used per day)
- Cough worse in the morning
- Deep breaths exacerbate cough
- Denies hemoptysis, rhinorrhea, sore throat, fever, chills, night sweats
- Reports fatigue and decreased energy over the last few months
- Dyspnea on exertion (DOE) - trouble breathing when walking a few blocks or climbing a few flights of stairs
- Nocturia
- Poor sleep
- Unintentional 5 lb weight loss over the last few months
- Good appetite, eating 1-2 meals per day with snacks, diet includes variety of meats and vegetables
- Denies recent infections, ill contacts, constipation, diarrhea, chest pain, palpitations

PMHx:
- Hypertension (HTN)
- Medications: lisinopril (HTN)
- Surgical Hx: Cesarean section
- Denies allergies
- Father had MI at age 78 and passed away from it, no family history of cancer or asthma

Social Hx:
- Lives in apartment with husband and daughter 
- Works at local grocery store
- No regular exercise
- Denies recreational drug use
- EtOH: 5-6 oz glass of wine 3-4 nights/week
- Tobacco: 1-2 PPD for nearly 40 years

Objective:
- Vitals not provided
- Physical exam not performed

Assessment & Plan:
1. Chronic cough
- Likely COPD given long smoking history and progressive dyspnea
- Differential includes chronic bronchitis, emphysema, bronchiectasis, ACE inhibitor cough
- Spirometry to assess for obstructive lung disease
- Chest X-ray to evaluate for hyperinflation, bullae, bronchiectasis
- Consider trial off lisinopril given cough is a known side effect
- Smoking cessation counseling and resources, goal quit date
- Prescribe nicotine replacement therapy and/or varenicline to aid in cessation
- Pulmonary referral if cough persists despite smoking cessation and trial off ACE inhibitor

2. Hypertension
- Continue lisinopril
- Encourage lifestyle modifications - low sodium diet, regular aerobic exercise
- Check BMP to assess renal function and electrolytes
- Follow up in 3 months to recheck BP
