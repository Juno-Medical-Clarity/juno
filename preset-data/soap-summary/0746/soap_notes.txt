# Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SubashNeupane/dataset_SOAP_summary

Subjective:
- Patient is a 13-year-old male.
- He has a diagnosis of OCD (Osteochondritis Dissecans) in the left knee based on an MRI from Sierra Pacific Orthopedic Group.
- Family is seeking a second opinion regarding the treatment plan.
- Concerns regarding the risks of surgery, specifically the risk of infection.

Objective:
- MRI shows OCD in the left knee.
- Indication of gross instability of the fragment.

Assessment:
- Diagnosis: Osteochondritis Dissecans (OCD) in the left knee.
- Clinical impression suggests the need for surgical intervention due to instability.

Plan:
- Proceed with surgery on the left knee.
- Discussed risks of the procedure including infection (less than 1%), bleeding, changes in sensation and motion, failure to relieve pain or restore cartilage, possible need for another procedure, and risk of early arthritis.
- Antibiotics will be used to control for infection.
- Patient will be under anesthesia and will not feel pain during the procedure.
