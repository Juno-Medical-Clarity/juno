- No fevers, chills, recent illness, weight loss, loss of appetite, numbness, tingling, weakness, diarrhea, constipation or urinary changes

SocHx:

Objective:

Assessment:

Plan:
- X-ray of right shoulder (compare to previous)
- Consider ultrasound if X-ray inconclusive
- Bloodwork (routine and inflammatory markers)
- Physiotherapy (emphasize importance of completing full course)

- Consider corticosteroid injection if conservative management ineffective
