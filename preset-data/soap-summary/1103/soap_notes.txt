# SOAP Notes — Sample 1103
# Dataset: https://huggingface.co/datasets/SubashNeupane/dataset_SOAP_summary

Subjective:
- Patient is a 5.5-year-old boy.
- Complains of a lot of pain in both feet.
- Observed that his feet rotate inwards.
- Family is concerned about the condition and urgency for surgical intervention.
- Inquiry made about the risks of infection related to surgery.
- Concern about the ability to walk post-surgery.

Objective:
- Physical examination shows rocker bottom feet.

Assessment:
- Diagnosis: Rocker bottom feet.
- Surgical recommendation: Subtalar arthrodesis, possible autograft, and lengthening of the Achilles tendon.

Plan:
- Proceed with the surgery as soon as possible.
- Use prophylactic antibiotics to minimize the risk of infection (historically low risk, less than 1%).
- Post-operative care will involve a period of nonweightbearing.
- Discussed general risks associated with surgery, including anesthesia risks, infection, bleeding, changes in sensation and motion, hardware failure, and potential need for further procedures.
