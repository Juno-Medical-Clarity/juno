Flumazenil was associated with a beneficial effect on hepatic encephalopathy when including all randomised clinical trials (RR 0. 75, 95% CI 0. 71 to 0. 80; 824 participants; nine randomised clinical trials; low quality evidence), or just the trial at low risk of bias (RR 0. 78, 95% CI 0. 72 to 0. 84; 527 participants).
