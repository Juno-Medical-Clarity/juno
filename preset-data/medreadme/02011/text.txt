The within-trial health economic analysis found that incremental costs were lower in the intervention group than in the control group (mean difference-PS111, 95% CI-PS384 to PS136) and the quality-adjusted life-years (QALYs) were higher (mean difference 0. 02 QALYs, 95% CI 0. 000 to 0. 044 QALYs), meaning that the HeLP-Diabetes programme group dominated the control group.
