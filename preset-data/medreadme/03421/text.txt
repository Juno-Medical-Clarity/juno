For the primary outcomes, in an intention-to-treat analysis, when compared with treatment as usual, the therapy led to highly significant reductions in both worry [PSWQ: 6. 35, 95% confidence interval (CI) 3. 30 to 9. 40; p <0. 001] and the persecutory delusions (PSYRATS: 2. 08, 95% CI 0. 64 to 3. 51; p = 0. 005).
