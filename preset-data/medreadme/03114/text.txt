Our review compared the treatments axitinib (Inlyta (r), Pfizer Inc. , NY, USA), cabozantinib (Cabometyx (r), Ipsen, Slough, UK), everolimus (Afinitor (r), Novartis, Basel, Switzerland), nivolumab (Opdivo (r), Bristol-Myers Squibb, NY, USA), sunitinib (Sutent (r), Pfizer, Inc. , NY, USA) and best supportive care (BSC) to help NHS services choose the most effective option.
