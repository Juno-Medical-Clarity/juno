There was a reduction in the risk of head injury death with tranexamic acid in those with mild to moderate head injury (166/2846 vs. 207/2769; risk ratio 0. 78, 95% confidence interval 0. 64 to 0. 95), but in those with severe head injury (689/1739 vs. 685/1710; risk ratio 0. 99, 95% confidence interval 0. 91 to 1. 07) there was no apparent reduction (p-value for heterogeneity = 0. 030).
