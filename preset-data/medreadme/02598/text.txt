Severe anaemia (risk ratio [RR] 2. 74, 95% CI 1. 54 to 4. 88) and thrombocytopenia (RR 8. 09, 95% CI 3. 93 to 16. 67) were significantly more common with PLD / carbo, whereas alopecia (RR 0. 09, 95% CI 0. 06 to 0. 14) and severe neurotoxicity (RR 0. 09, 95% CI 0. 01 to 0. 66) were significantly more common with PAC / carbo.
