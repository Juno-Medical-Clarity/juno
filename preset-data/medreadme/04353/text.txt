In multivariable multinomial regression analyses, exposure to the intervention was associated with a greater likelihood of a large increase in the proportion of commuting trips involving any active travel [adjusted relative risk ratio (RRR) 1. 80, 95% confidence interval (CI) 1. 27 to 2. 55], of a large decrease in the proportion of trips made entirely by car (RRR 2. 09, 95% CI 1. 35 to 3. 21), and of an increase in weekly cycle commuting time (RRR 1. 34, 95% CI 1. 03 to 1. 76).
