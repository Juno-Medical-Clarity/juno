It was found that, compared with the placebo group, a higher proportion of babies were extubated at day 7 of life [5/8 (62. 5%) in the very low-dose dexamethasone group vs. 2/6 (33. 3%) in the placebo group] and duration of invasive ventilation was lower (a median of 23 days for the very low-dose dexamethasone group vs. a median of 31 days for the placebo group) in the very low-dose dexamethasone group.
