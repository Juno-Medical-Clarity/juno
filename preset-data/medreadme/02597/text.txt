For the comparison PLD / carbo versus PAC / carbo (820 women; stages Ic to IV), no statistically significant differences in progression - free survival (PFS) (hazard ratio [HR] 1. 01, 95% confidence interval [CI] 0. 85 to 1. 19) or overall survival (OS) (HR 0. 94, 95% CI 0. 78 to 1. 13) were noted between study arms.
