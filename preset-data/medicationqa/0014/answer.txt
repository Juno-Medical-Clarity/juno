What are the ingredients in montelukast sodium tablets?

Active ingredient: montelukast sodium, USP

Inactive ingredients:

10 mg tablet: croscarmellose sodium, hydroxypropyl cellulose, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, and microcrystalline cellulose. The film coating contains: black iron oxide, hydroxypropyl cellulose, hypromellose, red iron oxide, titanium dioxide, and yellow iron oxide.
