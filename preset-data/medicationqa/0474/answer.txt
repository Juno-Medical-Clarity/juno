PERCOCET - oxycodone hydrochloride and acetaminophen tablet. Each tablet, for oral administration, contains oxycodone hydrochloride and acetaminophen in the following strengths: Oxycodone Hydrochloride, USP          10 mg*
Acetaminophen, USP         325 mg
*10 mg oxycodone HCl is equivalent to 8.9637 mg of oxycodone.
