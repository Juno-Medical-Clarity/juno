Enoxaparin Sodium Injection is available in two concentrations:
100 mg/mL Concentration
  30 and 40 mg prefilled syringes, and 60, 80, and 100 mg graduated prefilled syringes each contain 10 mg enoxaparin sodium per 0.1 mL Water for Injection.
150 mg/mL Concentration
  120 and 150 mg graduated prefilled syringes contain 15 mg enoxaparin sodium per 0.1 mL Water for Injection.
