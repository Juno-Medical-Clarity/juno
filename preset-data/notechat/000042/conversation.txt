17. Doctor: I see. And have you been tested for any hereditary thrombophilic disorders like protein S deficiency, protein C deficiency, factor V Leiden mutation, prothrombin-mutation, or antiphospholipid syndrome?

18. Patient: Yes, I have been tested and those disorders have been ruled out in both me and my mother.

19. Doctor: Okay, and can you tell me about your latest blood analysis results in the emergency department?

20. Patient: Yes, my D-dimers were elevated to 25 mg/l FEU and my C-reactive protein was 184 mg/l. My antithrombin activity was also reduced to 61% on admission. A vascular ultrasound examination confirmed the clinically suspected thrombosis of the right lower extremity involving the external iliac, common and superficial femoral as well as the popliteal vein. I was treated with continuous infusion of unfractionated heparin at a therapeutic dose including several bolus administrations and antithrombin (4000 IE).
