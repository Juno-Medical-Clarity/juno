History of Present Illness
Donna Sandboxtest is a 41-year-old female presenting for urgent evaluation. Review of the clinical documentation reveals a complex presentation characterized by new-onset nausea, "profound fatigue," and amenorrhea. Given her history of Hyperemesis Gravidarum, there is significant concern for early pregnancy. The patient reports high anxiety regarding her current medication regimen, specifically the use of Paroxetine (Paxil) 10 mg daily. She is aware of the FDA Category D status and potential teratogenic risks (cardiac defects) associated with first-trimester exposure and requests immediate guidance.

Respiratory review identifies a **High Priority Clinical Alert**. The patient reports her asthma is sub-optimally controlled, describing "intermittent wheezing" despite the use of Albuterol sulfate. This suggests a failure of current monotherapy and necessitates an escalation of care consistent with GINA guidelines.

Cardiovascular review identifies a **Significant Clinical Discrepancy**. While the patient’s office blood pressure is elevated today at 160/90 mmHg (2025-12-08) and was 160/90 mmHg on 2025-09-09, she reports home readings averaging 120/70 mmHg. This liability suggests either "White Coat Hypertension" or masked uncontrolled hypertension requiring ambulatory verification to prevent end-organ damage.

A comprehensive review of the medication profile identifies multiple **Critical Safety Discrepancies**:
1.  **Teratogenic Risk:** The patient is on Paroxetine (Paxil), an SSRI with known fetal risks (Category D), while presenting with amenorrhea and nausea. This poses an immediate risk of congenital malformations if pregnancy is confirmed.
2.  **Guideline-Directed Therapy Gap:** The patient is utilizing a Short-Acting Beta Agonist (Albuterol) as monotherapy despite reporting wheezing and poor control. Current guidelines (GINA/NAEPP) recommend Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS) for symptom control to reduce exacerbation risk.

*   *Interpretation:* Amenorrhea + Nausea + Fatigue in 41F is pregnancy until proven otherwise.
