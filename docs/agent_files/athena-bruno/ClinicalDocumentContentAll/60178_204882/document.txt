History of Present Illness
Donna Sandboxtest is a 41-year-old female presenting for urgent evaluation. A review of the clinical record identifies an established history of Hyperemesis Gravidarum during prior pregnancies. The patient reports a recent onset of nausea, profound fatigue, and irregular menses, raising significant anxiety regarding potential pregnancy.

A comprehensive review of the patient's medication profile identifies a **Critical Safety Discrepancy**. The patient is currently prescribed Paroxetine (Paxil) 10 mg daily for anxiety. Paroxetine is an FDA Category D agent associated with congenital malformations (specifically cardiac septal defects) if taken during the first trimester. The patient expresses significant distress regarding these risks should pregnancy be confirmed.

Concurrently, the patient reports intermittent wheezing and relies on Albuterol monotherapy, indicating sub-optimal asthma control. Regarding cardiovascular health, there is a notable discrepancy between objective data points: today's office blood pressure is elevated at 160/90 mmHg, while the patient's reported home logs average 120/70 mmHg, suggesting possible "white coat" phenomenon vs. labile hypertension.

Medication reconciliation confirms the ongoing use of Paroxetine and Albuterol. There are no reports of chest pain, lower extremity edema, or severe headache.
