ASSESSMENT
**1. High Risk Medication Use / Potential Pregnancy (ICD-10: Z79.899, O09.93)**
*Status: Active / Critical Safety Review*
A comprehensive review of the medication profile identifies a **Critical Safety Discrepancy**. The patient is actively taking Paroxetine 10 mg daily (FDA Pregnancy Category D) while taking Prenatal Vitamins with a history of Hyperemesis gravidarum. Paroxetine use in the first trimester is associated with an increased risk of congenital cardiac malformations (ACOG Practice Bulletin No. 92).
*   **Action:** STAT Quantitative Beta-hCG ordered. If positive, immediate cross-taper to Sertraline 25 mg is required to mitigate teratogenic risk while maintaining psychiatric stability.

**2. Hypertensive Disorder, Unspecified (ICD-10: I10, HCC: 85)**
*Status: Uncontrolled / Erratic*
Cardiovascular review identifies a **High Priority Clinical Alert**. The patient exhibits significant blood pressure liability (Range: 120/70 to 160/90 mmHg). This requires immediate differentiation between essential hypertension, white coat effect, or gestational hypertension (if hCG positive).
*   **Guideline Alignment:** Per ACC/AHA 2017 Guidelines, confirmation of diagnosis requires out-of-office measurement.
*   **Action:** Home Blood Pressure Monitoring (HBPM) log ordered for 7 days. Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP) ordered to evaluate renal function (BUN/Cr).

**3. Mild Intermittent Asthma (ICD-10: J45.20, HCC: 111)**
*Status: Sub-optimally Managed*
Review of current therapy reveals the patient is on SABA monotherapy (Albuterol). This represents a **Safety Gap** per GINA 2024 guidelines, which state that SABA-only treatment increases the risk of severe exacerbations and is no longer recommended for adults.
*   **Guideline Alignment:** GINA 2024 recommends containing inhaled corticosteroid (ICS) in the reliever or as daily maintenance.
*   **Action:** Initiate Budesonide 90 mcg 2 puffs BID (low-dose ICS) to close this safety gap.
