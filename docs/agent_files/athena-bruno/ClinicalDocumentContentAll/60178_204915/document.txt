**SUBJECTIVE:**
Patient is a 41-year-old female with a history of Hypertensive Disorder, Mild Intermittent Asthma, and Allergic Rhinitis.

**Chief Complaint:** New-onset nausea, fatigue, amenorrhea, and anxiety regarding medication safety in potential pregnancy.

**History of Present Illness:**
Patient presents for urgent evaluation of profound fatigue, new-onset nausea, and irregular menses (amenorrhea), which has raised significant suspicion for early pregnancy. She has a history of Hyperemesis Gravidarum during prior pregnancy. She is currently taking Paroxetine (Paxil) 10 mg daily for anxiety and expresses high levels of distress regarding its FDA Category D status and potential teratogenic risks (cardiac malformations).
Regarding her asthma, she reports intermittent wheezing and currently utilizes Albuterol monotherapy, which suggests sub-optimal control.
Regarding hypertension, there is a discrepancy in blood pressure readings; office reading today is elevated at 160/90 mmHg, while patient reports home readings averaging 120/70 mmHg. She denies chest pain or shortness of breath at rest. Functional status is impacted by anxiety and fatigue.

**ASSESSMENT:**
**1. Potential Pregnancy / High-Risk Medication Use (ICD-10: Z32.01, Z79.899)**
Patient presents with symptoms highly suggestive of pregnancy (amenorrhea, nausea, fatigue) and history of Hyperemesis Gravidarum. Current use of **Paroxetine (Paxil)** poses a safety signal (FDA Category D) due to risk of cardiovascular malformations if continued in the first trimester (ACOG Guidelines). A conditional cross-taper plan is required immediately pending STAT hCG results.
*   *Validation Alert:* No validation alerts - data consistent.

**2. Mild Intermittent Asthma, Sub-optimally Controlled (ICD-10: J45.20)**
Reliance on SABA (Albuterol) monotherapy with continued wheezing indicates a gap in care. **GINA 2024 Guidelines** no longer recommend SABA-only treatment for safety reasons and recommend symptom-driven or daily low-dose ICS.
*   *Plan:* Initiate Budesonide.

**3. Hypertensive Disorder, Unspecified (ICD-10: I10)**
Office BP (160/90 mmHg) meets Stage 2 Hypertension criteria per **ACC/AHA 2017 Guidelines**, but home readings (120/70 mmHg) suggest White Coat Hypertension.
*   *Plan:* Verify with Home Blood Pressure Monitoring (HBPM) before escalating antihypertensives, especially given pregnancy differential.

**4. Health Maintenance**
Patient is due for Influenza vaccination and Mammography screening.

**PLAN:**
**Diagnostic Plan:**
1.  **STAT Quantitative Beta-hCG**: To confirm pregnancy status immediately.
2.  **Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP) & Lipid Panel**: Baseline metabolic assessment.
3.  **Home Blood Pressure Monitoring (HBPM)**: Patient to log BP BID x 7 days to rule out White Coat Hypertension.

**Therapeutic Plan:**
1.  **Asthma Management:**
    -   Initiate **Budesonide 90 mcg**, 2 puffs BID.
    -   Continue Albuterol PRN for breakthrough symptoms.
    -   *Guideline:* Aligns with GINA 2024 Track 2 recommendations.
2.  **Obstetric/Psychiatric Management (Conditional):**
    -   Initiate **Prenatal Vitamins** 1 tablet daily immediately.
    -   **IF hCG POSITIVE:** Decrease Paroxetine to 5 mg daily x 7 days, then DISCONTINUE. Initiate **Sertraline (Zoloft) 25 mg** daily (Category C, preferred SSRI in pregnancy per ACOG/APA).
    -   **IF hCG NEGATIVE:** Continue Paroxetine 10 mg daily. Counsel on reliable contraception.
3.  **Health Maintenance:**
    -   **Influenza Vaccine (Quadrivalent)** administered today.
    -   Referral placed for **Mammography**.

**Patient Education:**
-   Counseled extensively on the risks of Paroxetine in early pregnancy vs. risks of untreated anxiety.
-   Reviewed proper inhaler technique for Budesonide.
-   Educated on signs of preeclampsia (headache, vision changes) given hypertensive history.

**Follow-up:**
-   24-hour phone follow-up to review STAT hCG results and confirm medication plan.
-   2-week in-office visit to review BP logs and asthma response.
