Assessment
1. High Risk Medication Use / Potential Pregnancy (ICD-10: Z79.899, Z31.89)
A critical safety alert exists regarding the concomitant use of Paroxetine (SSRI) and Prenatal Vitamins. Paroxetine poses a specific risk for cardiovascular malformations (e.g., VSD/ASD) if taken during the first trimester (FDA Category D). If pregnancy is confirmed via STAT HCG, immediate cross-titration is required. ACOG guidelines generally favor Sertraline or Fluoxetine over Paroxetine in pregnancy due to the safer side effect profile.

2. Hypertensive Disorder, Unspecified (ICD-10: I10, HCC: 85)
Blood pressure control is erratic and currently uncontrolled with a recent in-clinic reading of 160/90 mmHg. This meets the threshold for Stage 2 Hypertension per ACC/AHA 2017 guidelines. However, pharmacotherapy initiation is complex due to potential pregnancy (ACEi/ARBs contraindicated) and the erratic nature of readings. Accurate diagnosis requires out-of-office confirmation to rule out white-coat effect versus sustained hypertension.

3. Mild Intermittent Asthma, Sub-optimally Managed (ICD-10: J45.20)
The patient is currently on SABA monotherapy (Albuterol). This deviates from GINA 2024 guidelines, which recommend against SABA-only treatment due to increased risk of severe exacerbations. Evidence supports the inclusion of an inhaled corticosteroid (ICS)-containing controller, either as needed (ICS-formoterol) or daily low-dose ICS, to reduce risk.
