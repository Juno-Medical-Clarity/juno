## 5. Core Product Story — "How It Works"

**H2:** From clinical note to patient action, in four steps.

**Step 1 — Ingest**
> Juno takes what your clinicians already produce — EHR notes, after-visit summaries, discharge instructions, or a visit transcript — no new documentation work required.

**Step 2 — Translate, source-grounded**
> Every plain-language summary is tied explicitly back to what the clinician actually wrote or said. Juno translates, it doesn't interpret — no added medical advice, and every statement is labeled "provider said / record says / pending."

**Step 3 — Deliver and adapt**
> The patient (and, with permission, their caregiver) gets a summary at their reading level, in their language, with medication changes flagged, an action-item checklist, and a chat to ask follow-up questions — delivered by SMS, WhatsApp, email, or portal link, no app download required.

**Step 4 — Track and report back**
> As patients engage — opening the summary, completing tasks, flagging confusion, asking questions — Juno rolls that activity into a clinic-facing dashboard: understanding scores, missed-action alerts, and unresolved questions routed to staff only when a human is actually needed.

**Supporting credibility line (optional, under the steps):**
> Ambiguous or higher-risk questions escalate to your staff automatically — Juno is designed to close the gap between what was said and what gets done, not to replace clinical judgment.

**Data source:** Feature map lines 36–68 (ingestion via EHR/PDF/transcript, source-grounding rules, delivery channels, chat + escalation, clinic dashboard: understanding score, missed-action queue, unresolved-question routing).

## 6. Feature Grid

*(6 cards, icon + short title + one sentence — grid format per structure doc's recommendation)*

1. **Plain-Language Summaries** — Clinical notes and discharge instructions rewritten in clear, everyday language, adapted to each patient's health-literacy level.
2. **Medication Clarity** — What changed, what was added or stopped, and why — using only your clinicians' own guidance, never invented advice.
3. **Multilingual by Default** — The same source-grounded facts, delivered in the patient's own language.
4. **Action-Item Checklists** — Follow-up tasks with due dates and status, so nothing falls through after the visit — for the patient and, separately, for their caregiver.
5. **Caregiver Sharing** — A permissioned, revocable link gives family caregivers the same summary and checklist, mirrored and auditable.
6. **Clinic Analytics Dashboard** — Engagement, understanding scores, confusion flags, and missed-action alerts — so your team knows exactly who needs a follow-up call before they miss one.

*(Optional 7th/8th card if room allows:)*

7. **Source-Grounded, No Hallucination** — Every statement traces back to a specific note or record — ambiguous questions escalate to your staff instead of getting guessed at.
8. **No New App Required** — Delivered by SMS, WhatsApp, email, or your existing portal link.

**Data source:** All eight pulled directly from the Patient-facing, Caregiver-facing, Clinic-facing, and Safety & Compliance feature lists in overview lines 36–68.
